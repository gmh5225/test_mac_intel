**English.** The two finite-probe failures assumed that a 5 ms slice necessarily executes a guest store. A timer can expire before the first guest instruction; the assertion triggered GoogleTest SIGTRAP and both children retired. The revised probe retains one total deadline while allowing empty slices and requires a fresh store witness. Executor reuse retains the VM and executor threads across iterations; cancellation still recreates the vCPU. These controls do not establish a production fix or full CPU/Darwin acceptance. The initial 100-loop probe lacked the later exit allowlist; its saved returns were audited separately. Pending runs are not included as passes.
